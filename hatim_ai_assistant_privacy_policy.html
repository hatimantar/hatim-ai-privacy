Privacy Policy – Hatim AI Assistant

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Hatim AI Assistant is a personal AI-powered assistant accessible through WhatsApp. This policy explains how information is processed when someone interacts with the assistant.

2. Information Processed

Depending on how the assistant is used, it may process the sender's WhatsApp phone number and profile information made available by WhatsApp, message content, and information included in requests such as questions, instructions, and reminders. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to receive and respond to WhatsApp messages, understand user requests, generate AI-powered responses, and provide features explicitly requested by the user.

4. Third-Party Services

Meta / WhatsApp: WhatsApp messages are transmitted through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

OpenRouter and its model providers: Message content may be sent to OpenRouter and the AI model provider selected for a request to generate a response. Their own terms, privacy policies, and data-retention practices apply.

5. Storage and Security

The assistant currently runs through a locally operated Python application. Message content may appear temporarily in application logs while requests are processed. The application may be extended to store tasks, reminders, or other information; this policy should be updated before such storage is enabled. Reasonable measures are taken to protect API credentials and restrict access to the application, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Sharing

Information is shared with Meta/WhatsApp and OpenRouter or the selected model provider only as needed to deliver the assistant's functionality. The administrator does not sell personal information.

7. Retention and Deletion

The application is currently configured for message processing and does not intentionally maintain a permanent archive of message conversations. Technical logs and third-party service records may be retained according to the relevant provider's practices. For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning information controlled by the administrator, contact the administrator using the contact details provided with the service.

8. Children's Privacy

The assistant is not designed specifically for children. Do not provide personal information about a child unless you are authorized to do so.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the assistant's features, data handling, or service providers change. The updated version will be published at the same policy address.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Hatim AI Assistant administrator at the email address provided by the administrator when making the assistant available.
